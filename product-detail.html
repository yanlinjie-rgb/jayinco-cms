产品说明Product Description

本产品为JAYINCO佳盈盛电子自主研发生产的XH系列线对板连接器。采用高品质磷青铜接触件配合锡铅镀层，确保长期稳定的电气连接性能。绝缘体选用PA66尼龙加玻纤材料，具备优异的机械强度和耐热性能。This product is a self-developed XH series wire-to-board connector by JAYINCO Electronics. It uses high-quality phosphor bronze contacts with tin-lead plating to ensure long-term stable electrical connection performance. The insulator is made of PA66 nylon with glass fiber, offering excellent mechanical strength and heat resistance.
