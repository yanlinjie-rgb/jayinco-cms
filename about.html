公司简介Company Profile

精工品质，连接全球Precision Quality, Global Connections

佳盈盛电子（JAYINCO），从电子贸易公司起家，拥有多年技术经验累计转型的电子连接器制造厂。JAYINCO (JiayingSheng Electronics) started as an electronics trading company and has evolved into a professional connector manufacturer backed by years of accumulated technical expertise.

产品初期代理日本的 ISA 98PIN SLOT 及 PCI SLOT 等板卡连接器，渐进转型生产各类连接器及特殊客制化产品。We began as a distributor for Japanese ISA 98PIN SLOT and PCI SLOT board-to-board connectors, then progressively transitioned into manufacturing a full range of connectors and custom-designed products.

公司主要产品有各类连接器、接线端子和线缆组件，产品广泛应用于 PC 机、电子电器、通信、航天航海等领域，为客户提供最精密可靠的连接方案。Our main product lines include various connectors, terminals and wire harness assemblies — widely used in PCs, consumer electronics, communications, aerospace, marine and other industries, delivering the most precise and reliable interconnection solutions to our customers.
