Global Connections

佳盈盛电子（JAYINCONN），从电子贸易起家，拥有多年技术经验积累转型的专业连接器制造商。产品初期代理日本 ISA 98PIN SLOT 及 PCI SLOT 等板卡连接器，渐进转型生产各类连接器及特殊客制化产品。JAYINCONN started as an electronics trading company and has evolved into a professional connector manufacturer backed by years of accumulated technical expertise. We began distributing Japanese ISA 98PIN SLOT and PCI SLOT board connectors, then progressively transitioned into manufacturing a full range of connectors and custom-designed products.

主要产品有各类连接器、接线端子和线缆组件，广泛应用于 PC 机、电子电器、通信、航天航海等领域。Our main product lines include various connectors, terminals and wire harness assemblies — widely used in PCs, consumer electronics, communications, aerospace and marine industries.
